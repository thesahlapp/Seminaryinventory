# Inventory app: team guide

A quick guide to the Qalam Seminary inventory app. It works on any phone or computer.

**Address:** <https://seminaryinventory.vercel.app>

## Signing in

1. You'll get an email invitation. Click the link and choose a password.
2. After that, sign in at the address above with your email and password.
3. Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, tap **Forgot password? Email me a reset link**.

**Put it on your phone** so it opens like an app:

- **iPhone:** open the address in **Safari** → tap **Share** (square with an arrow) →
  **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
- **Android:** open the address in **Chrome** → tap **⋮** (top right) → **Install app**
  (or **Add to Home screen**) → **Install**.

These steps are also under **My settings** in the app (tap ☰, then your name).

What you can do depends on your role:

| | Admin | Editor | Viewer |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Look things up, scan codes | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add and edit items, change quantities, move stock, check gear in and out, count stock | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Costs and value, reports, purchase orders, CSV import, team, settings | ✓ | | |

## Add an item

1. Tap **Inventory**, then **Add item** (or **Add item** on the Home screen).
2. Fill in the **name** and **category**. SKU, description and notes are optional.
3. Clothing? Tick **This item comes in sizes**. You'll get a row for each size.
4. Add photos: **📷 Take photo** uses your camera, **Choose photos** picks from your
   gallery. You can add several. The first photo is the cover; drag to reorder.
5. Optional: set a **low stock minimum** so you're warned when it runs low, and tick
   **Can be checked out** for gear people borrow.
6. Tap **Create item**, then enter how many you have at each location.

## Change a quantity

On the item page (or in the Inventory list), every number has **−** and **+**:

- Tap **+** when stock comes in, **−** when it's used or given out. It saves by itself.
- Tap the number itself to type the exact count (for example after counting a shelf).
- To give a reason (damaged, lost, returned…) or add a note, use the form under the grid
  on the item page.

Every change is recorded in **History** with your name and the time, so nothing is lost if
you make a mistake. Just set it back.

**Moving stock between locations:** tap **Move stock**, pick the item, the size, where it's
coming from and going to, and how many.

## Scan a QR code

Every item and location has a QR code (admins print them from **Print labels**).

1. Tap the round **Scan** button at the bottom of the screen (or top, on a computer).
2. Allow the camera the first time.
3. Point at the label. The item opens, with quick **−** / **+** buttons.

A location label opens that location's list of everything stored there. No camera? Tap
**Search instead**.

## Check gear out and in

For gear people borrow, like cameras, tripods or event kits:

**Check out**
1. Tap **Checked out**, then **Check out**. (Or open the item and tap **Check out**.)
2. Add the items and how many, or switch to **A whole kit**.
3. Enter **who is taking them**, the **due back** date and, if you like, the project.
4. Tap **Check out**. The items show as "out" and aren't counted as available.

**Check in**
1. Tap **Checked out** and open the check-out.
2. For each item, enter how many came back **Good**, **Damaged** or **Missing**, with a
   note if something's wrong.
3. Tap **Check in**. Good and damaged items go back into stock; missing items are
   recorded as lost.

Anything past its due date is shown in red, and the borrower (if they're on the team) and
the admins get a reminder email.

## Other things you'll see

- **Home:** totals, low stock, what's checked out or overdue, and recent changes.
- **Low stock:** items at or below their minimum get a red **Low** badge. Filter the
  Inventory list with **Low stock** to see them all.
- **Comments:** each item page has a comment thread. Type **@** and a name to notify
  someone. The 🔔 bell shows your notifications.
- **Stock counts:** tap ☰ (or **More** on a computer) → **Stock counts**, pick a location
  and tap **Start count**. Several people
  can count at once; an admin reviews the differences and applies them.
- **Dark mode:** **My settings → Appearance**.

## Something wrong?

- **A number looks off:** open the item and check its **History** to see who changed what
  and when.
- **"You're offline":** you can look around, but changes won't save until you're back
  online.
- **Can't do something you need to:** ask an admin to change your role on the **Team** page.
